Deltaspect: live stock perp charts

A free, real-time dashboard for stock perpetuals: TSLA, AMD, AAPL, SpaceX (SPCX), PLTR, NVDA, META and more.

  • Overview table with price, 24h change, distance from the moving average on each timeframe, oracle price, basis and 24h range.
  • Live 1-minute, 1-hour and 1-day candle charts with volume and a 90-period EMA.
  • Your own watchlist: add, remove and reorder tickers, saved in your browser.
  • Runs entirely in the browser on a public market data API. No account, no API key.
